On the day

  • Nothing new on race day: the same shoes, kit, breakfast and gels you used on your long runs.
  • Breakfast about three hours before the start; a gel or a few sips of sports drink 15 minutes before.
  • Start in your pen for your finish time and let the first 5 km feel easy. The race starts at 30 km.
  • Take carried gels just before a station, then wash them down with its water; take course gels at the station, with its water.
