# Race Preparation Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fuelling notes on long runs of 90 minutes or more, a "Fuelled as planned" tick, and a Barcelona
race-day pacing and fuelling page.

**Architecture:** A new pure module `app/fuel.py` holds the gel schedule, the long-run note and the race plan.
`build_view` attaches `wo["fuel"]` (note text) and `wo["fuelled"]` (tick) to long runs; the tick is a new stored
fact (`fuel_logs`). A new `GET /race` page; links from This week (taper and race week) and the Plan page.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, Jinja2, SQLite, pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-race-prep-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Gel schedule: first at 45 min, then every 35 min, none in the last 10 minutes; only for runs of 90 min or more.
- Long-run duration = planned km × `s["paces"]["long"]`. Race duration = `s["paces"]["marathon"]` × 42.195.
- Race pacing: first 5 km at MP + 5 s/km; the rest at (MP × 42.195 − 5 × (MP + 5)) / 37.195 s/km.
- Settings defaults: `race_stations` = [5, 10, 12.5, 15, 17.5, 20, 22.5, 25, 27.5, 30, 32.5, 35, 37.5, 40];
  `race_gel_stations` = [15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 37.5]. Labelled "From recent editions: check the 2027 course guide."
- Plan km, paces and `.fit` files don't change. No JavaScript; POST then 303. Phone-first, design tokens.

## Review Focus

1. **A long run trimmed by the check-in or the missed-week guard** below 90 min: the note follows the trimmed km
   (no note, or fewer gels). → Task 2 `test_trimmed_long_run_follows_its_km`.
2. **A moved long run**: the note and the tick follow it to its new day. → Task 3 `test_tick_follows_a_moved_long_run`.
3. **A tick on a day with no long run, or a future day**: refused, nothing stored. → Task 3 `test_tick_refused_without_a_long_run`.
4. **A slow goal or result** (MP 6:09): the race plan stays sane, more gels, still none in the last 10 minutes,
   no station used twice. → Task 1 `test_race_plan_at_a_slower_pace`.
5. **Race week's long run is the race**: no fuelling note on the race workout; the race page is its fuelling.
   → Task 2 `test_race_has_no_long_run_note`.

**Files:** Create `app/fuel.py`; Test `tests/test_fuel.py`

**Interfaces — Produces:**
- `gel_minutes(duration_min: float) -> list[int]`: `[]` under 90; else 45, 80, 115, … while `t <= duration_min - 10`.
- `long_run_note(km: float, mp_km: float, long_pace: float) -> str | None`:
  "Fuel practice: a Finisher gel at 45 and 80 min (2 gels), with water." (minutes joined "45, 80, 115 and 150");
  with `mp_km > 0` append " Take the gel before the marathon-pace part as on race day, and keep taking them at goal pace."
- `race_plan(mp: int, stations: list[float], gel_stations: list[float]) -> dict`:

  21.0975 ("Half") and 42.195 ("Finish"), sorted by km. Gels due before `gel_stations[0]` are carried; later ones
